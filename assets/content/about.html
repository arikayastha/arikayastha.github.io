Tid bits about me

Currently, I am working on my PhD at Nokia Bell Labs and Télécom ParisTech under Camille Delezoide, Patricia Layec, Cedric Ware and Mounia Lourdiane. I completed my bachelor's in electronics engineering from Vidyalankar Institute of Technology, Mumbai, and went on to do my master's in electrical engineering, with a specialization in optical communication systems from Institut Polytechnique de Paris, Palaiseau. For my master's dissertation, I worked at Alcatel Submarine Networks, in the System Design team on experimental analysis of multicore fiber transmission systems in submarine environment, and then on attempting to model the noise figure of an EDFA, and evaluating the system impact of such a model. Over the years, academically I have been interested in multiple subjects ranging from control systems and robotics to quantum engineering and now onto optics.

I am someone who gets very easily bored, so as a result I also work equally hard to find hobbies I can throw at my brain to keep the mundane humdrum of my existence from taking over. I sometimes read books as a distraction, but honestly it is mostly as an addiction that my father disapproves of. I indulge myself in historical high fantasy, especially in Asian settings, slow-burn romances, and lots and lots of manga/manhua/manhwa. Aside from nerding and acting like a general weeb, I also spend a lot of brain juice learning different traditional instruments. So far I can play the bansuri (Indian bamboo flute), dizi (Chinese transverse bamboo flute), đàn tranh (Vietnamese zither), and am now counting all my pennies to fund and learn a guqin (Chinese zither). And of course, like any self respecting emo-kid, I spend a good amount of time right before submissions and exams writing poetry and prose and whatever other word vomit my brain decides to prioritize.
