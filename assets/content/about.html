Tid bits about me

Currently, I am working on finishing my PhD at Nokia Bell Labs, Télécom Paris and Télécom SudParis under the supervision of Camille Delezoide, Patricia Layec, Cedric Ware and Mounia Lourdiane. I did my bachelor's in electronics engineering from Vidyalankar Institute of Technology in Mumbai, India, and then went on to do my master's in electrical engineering, with a specialization in optical communication systems from Institut Polytechnique de Paris in Palaiseau, France.

For my master's dissertation, I worked at Alcatel Submarine Networks, in the System Design team on experimental analysis of multicore fiber transmission systems in submarine environment, and then on attempting to model the noise figure of a single stage EDFA when operated in non-ideal conditions, and then evaluating the system impact of such a model. Over the years, academically I have been interested in multiple subjects ranging from control systems and robotics to quantum engineering and now onto optics.

I am someone who gets very easily bored, so as a result I also work equally hard to find hobbies I can throw at my brain to keep the mundane humdrum of my existence from taking over. I sometimes read books as a distraction, but honestly it is mostly as an addiction that my father disapproves of. I indulge myself in historical high fantasy, especially in Asian settings, slow-burn romances, and lots and lots of manga/ manhua/ manhwa.

Aside from nerding and acting like a general weeb, I also spend a lot of brain juice learning different traditional instruments. So far I can play the bansuri which is an Indian bamboo flute, dizi and xiao which are Chinese transverse and vertical bamboo flutes respectively, and the shakuhachi which is a Japanese vertical bamboo flute that I am also learning classically. I am now counting all my pennies to fund and learn a guqin which is a Chinese 7-stringed zither.

And of course, like any self respecting emo-kid (if the landing page didn't clue you in already, here's the disclaimer), I spend a good amount of time right before submissions writing poetry and prose and whatever other word vomit my brain decides to prioritize instead.

You can find my writing projects at my poetry blog.

This website was built by me one obnoxiously hot French summer weekend, fueled solely by caffeine and boredom. I used Canva to create the artwork from the available library of vector graphics, but it is heavily inspired by what can be found on my desk (yes that includes the bird skull).

I know, that this website doesn't look like a traditional academic website, but I wanted it to have my personality; and although I try to remain quite professional here, I do tend to do incredibly wacky things on the design of the website itself, for eg: hovering on stuff will show you different things. There's really not a lot of reason other than that I personally find it hilarious, and no other website builder (like wordpress or squarespace) will allow me to do this without me giving up my entire inheritance T.T And because I think it would be a shame to show you who I am without a bit of the whimsy that makes me.
